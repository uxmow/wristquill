Getting Started

Wristquill uses your own Anthropic API key. Create one in the Anthropic Console, then paste it in Settings on your iPhone. It is sent to your Apple Watch automatically.

Who pays for the answers?

You do, through your Anthropic account. The credit ring in the app is an estimate based on the balance you enter and the usage counted on your devices.

The Watch does not show my chats.

Open Wristquill on both devices, keep them close, then tap Sync to Watch in Settings on the iPhone.

An answer was wrong or harmful.

Answers come from AI and can be wrong. Long-press the answer and choose Report a Response; this opens an email draft to us.
