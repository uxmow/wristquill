Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The Short Version

Wristquill has no server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The developer does not collect, see, store or sell any of your data.

What Stays on Your Devices

Your chats, attachments and settings are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Like other app data, they can be part of your device backups. Your API key is kept in the Keychain on each device and is never synced to iCloud.

What Is Sent to Anthropic

When you ask something, the conversation (your messages, photos and files) goes straight from your device to Anthropic's API with your own API key. If web search is on, Anthropic runs the searches. Anthropic handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy, not this one.

Between iPhone and Apple Watch

Chats, settings, your API key and the credit balance move between your iPhone and Apple Watch directly through Apple's Watch Connectivity. No other server is involved.

Apple Services

Siri, dictation and Scribble are provided by Apple and follow Apple's privacy policy. Read Aloud uses the voices on your device.

Reporting a Response

Report a Response only opens an email draft. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting a chat removes it from both devices. Clear Key removes your API key from both devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device; clear the key first to be sure it is also gone from the Keychain.

Age

Wristquill is meant for adults.

Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: wristquill@icloud.com.

Wristquill is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.
